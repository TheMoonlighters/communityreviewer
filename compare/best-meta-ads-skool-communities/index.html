Frequently asked questions

Which Skool community is best for learning Meta ads?

It depends on your stage. The Facebook Ads Blueprint suits ecommerce brand owners who want agency-grade campaign structure and internal systems. Meta Ads Mastery suits advertisers who want the highest volume of live coaching. Origins Ecommerce suits earlier-stage Shopify sellers who need the whole store playbook, not only ads. Ben Heath Meta Ads Academy suits people who mainly want a structured video course at the lowest price.

How much do Meta ads Skool communities cost?

The four communities compared here run from $49 to $497 per month: Ben Heath Meta Ads Academy at $49, Origins Ecommerce at $98, The Facebook Ads Blueprint at $297, and Meta Ads Mastery at $497. All are billed monthly. Prices are taken from each community's public Skool page in August 2026 and can change, so confirm before joining.

Are there free Skool communities for Facebook ads?

Yes. Free Skool communities covering Facebook and Meta ads do exist, and they can be a reasonable starting point for fundamentals. What free communities rarely include is structured curriculum, live coaching, or direct access to an experienced media buyer, which is what the paid communities on this page charge for.

Is Meta Ads Mastery worth $497 per month?

That depends entirely on how much of the coaching you use. Meta Ads Mastery includes three live calls per week plus same-day video answers, which is the most coaching access of the four communities here. If you attend consistently and manage meaningful ad spend, the cost can be justified. If you mostly want recorded training, cheaper options cover that.

Can you cancel a Skool community membership at any time?

All four communities on this page state on their public pages that membership is billed monthly and can be cancelled at any time, with no lock-in contract. Cancellation is handled inside Skool itself rather than through this site.

Do these Skool communities offer refunds or guarantees?

Refund terms differ by community and are set by each owner, not by Skool. Meta Ads Mastery publicly advertises a one-year money-back guarantee. The others advertise monthly billing with cancel-anytime terms rather than a stated refund window. Always check the current terms on the community's own page before paying.
